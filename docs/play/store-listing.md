# Store listing draft

**App name (30 chars):** Pratyahara: Pause the Scroll

**Short description (80 chars):** Limit Reels & Shorts, keep the rest of the app. Private, on-device, no ads.

**Full description:**

Lost an hour to Reels again? Pratyahara pauses only the short-video part of your apps, so you keep your feed, messages and work tools.

Named after pratyahara, the yogic practice of drawing the senses back from what pulls at them, it adds calm, honest friction at exactly the moment you'd keep scrolling.

• Limits only Reels (Instagram), Shorts (YouTube) and the TikTok video feed
• A daily budget, 30 minutes by default, counted only while you're actually watching
• Need more? Do 20 squats to earn 5 minutes. Your phone counts them.
• A short breathing pause before Reels opens, so every visit is a choice.
• Focus hours: Reels and Shorts stay closed at bedtime or study time.
• A home-screen widget with your minutes left.
• Raising your limit needs a real reason and a cooling-off wait; lowering it is instant
• One task each night, one honest yes or no the next day, and a streak for honesty
• A morning note from your past self
• Gentle design, dark mode, no guilt trips

Private by design: no internet access, no ads, no analytics, no account. Everything stays on your phone.

Accessibility permission: Pratyahara uses Android's Accessibility Service API only to recognise when Reels or Shorts is open in the apps you choose, count that time, and show a pause screen. It never reads or stores your messages or anything else on screen.

**Category:** Health & Fitness (alternative: Productivity)
**Content rating:** Everyone
**Target audience:** 18+ (avoids Families policy requirements for v1)
