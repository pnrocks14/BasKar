# Store listing

**App name (30 max):** BasKar: Stop the Scroll

**Short description (80 max):** Limit Reels and Shorts, keep the rest of the app. Private, offline, no ads.

**Full description:**

Bas kar! (बस कर) is Hindi for "enough, stop it". It's what a good friend says when one more reel turns into fifty.

BasKar limits only the short-video part of your apps, so your feed, messages and work tools keep working.

What it does
• Limits only Reels (Instagram), Shorts (YouTube) and the TikTok video feed
• A daily time limit, 30 minutes by default, counted only while you're actually watching
• A reel counter with a little brain that slowly goes from fresh to fried as the count climbs
• An optional daily reel limit: 30, 50 or 100 reels
• Need more? Do 20 squats to earn 5 minutes. Your phone counts them.
• A short breathing pause on your first visit of the day, and now and then after that
• Focus hours: Reels and Shorts stay closed at bedtime or study time
• Strict mode: lock your limits for 1, 3 or 7 days
• A weekly report every Sunday evening
• A home-screen widget and a small floating button with your time left
• Raising your limit needs a real reason and a cooling-off wait. Lowering it is instant.
• One task each night, an honest yes or no the next day, and a streak for honesty

Private by design: no internet access, no ads, no analytics, no account. Everything stays on your phone.

Accessibility permission: BasKar uses Android's Accessibility Service API only to recognise when Reels or Shorts is open in the apps you choose, count that time and the reels you watch, and show a pause screen. It never reads or stores your messages or anything else on screen, and nothing leaves your phone.

BasKar is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Instagram, Meta, YouTube, Google or TikTok. Those names are used only to say which apps BasKar works with.
